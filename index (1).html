Method

How a case runs

We use public block explorers and open forensic tools. Everything we publish can be checked by anyone.

Intake

A victim or researcher sends the scam site, deposit address and transaction hashes.

Trace

We map the wallet network and flag the consolidation wallets and exchange deposit addresses.

Report

We write a clear forensic report and submit it to exchange compliance teams and Chainabuse.

Publish

Confirmed scam addresses and domains go into a public list to warn the next victim.

We never ask for

  • Your seed phrase or private keys
  • Any payment to start or continue a case
  • Remote access to your device or wallet

Anyone who asks for these in our name is a scammer. Recovery-service scams target victims a second time.

What to expect

  • Tracing and a written report, free for individual victims
  • Submission to the exchanges and databases the funds touch
  • No promise of recovery. Freezing is each platform's decision
